Self Publishing

Self-Publishing on arxiv.org

Make sure your work is reproducible and usable by others. Protection against theft does no good if nobody ever sees or listens to your ideas. In addition to publishing a paper on arXiv, publish the code on github and submit a benchmark to https://paperswithcode.com/ that demonstrates why your approach is worth using / building upon! If you have code on github, a paper on arXiv, and a reproducible benchmark, then nobody will steal your idea because they will be too busy citing you! Also don't forget to submit to a conference. You can also get a Digital Object Indentifier (DOI) for the software / data at a place like zenodo.org - the benefit of this is that it gives you a timestamped citation. I used it to share a piece of software (and even add it to a line in my CV). Great for establishing provenance if you want to release your code and also maintain you were first.
