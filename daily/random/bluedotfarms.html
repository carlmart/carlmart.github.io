BlueDot
Blue Dot Farms is a community-contributed directory of independent U.S. farmers and ranchers who identify as supporting Democratic or progressive values, serving as a consumer movement to bypass traditionally conservative ("red") agricultural operations. The initiative allows farms to self-identify as "Blue Dot" or "Blue Farm" to signal they do not support MAGA ideology or the current administration's policies. bluedotfarms.org
